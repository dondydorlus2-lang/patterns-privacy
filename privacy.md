# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** July 26, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 26, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how Patterns (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use our habit-tracking app and related services (the “Service”).

## 1. Missouri & U.S. privacy framework

Missouri does not currently have a single comprehensive consumer privacy statute like some other states. Protections that commonly apply include:

- **Missouri Merchandising Practices Act (MMPA)** — fair and non-deceptive practices;
- **Missouri data breach notification law (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 407.1500)** — notice to affected Missouri residents without unreasonable delay if covered personal information is compromised;
- **Federal laws** such as COPPA (under 13), CAN-SPAM, and other rules that may apply by feature.

We voluntarily offer access, correction, and deletion controls to all users, including Missouri residents.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account & identity
- Email address, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), name/username you provide
- Optional profile fields (for example date of birth)
- Google account identifiers if you sign in with Google

### Habit & product data
- Habits, schedules, reminders, check-in logs, freezes, goals, media you attach
- Trips / travel mode details you enter
- Social features: friends, groups, challenges, encouragement messages you send
- Settings such as theme, notification preferences, and Premium status

### Device & technical data
- IP address, device/browser type, app version, approximate timezone
- Diagnostics and performance data
- Push subscription endpoints if you enable web push
- Analytics events (for example product usage via PostHog or similar)

### Payments
- Billing email, plan selection, and processor identifiers (Stripe and/or Wix; Apple/Google if store billing is used)
- We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers

### Calendar (optional)
- If you enable Google Calendar sync, we process the calendar tokens/events needed to create or update habit reminders you authorize

## 3. How we use information

- Provide, secure, and improve the Service (habits, streaks, reminders, social, travel)
- Authenticate users, verify email, and prevent abuse
- Process Premium subscriptions and customer support
- Send transactional emails (verification, password reset, important account notices)
- Optional product tips or marketing emails where permitted — you can unsubscribe
- Analyze aggregated usage to improve reliability and features
- Comply with law, including Missouri breach-notification duties if applicable

## 4. How we share information

We do **not** sell your personal information for money. We share data only as needed with:

- **Service providers** (hosting, auth, email, analytics, push, payment processors) under contractual confidentiality and security expectations
- **Other users you choose** (friends, groups, challenges) for social features
- **Legal / safety** when required by law, to protect rights, or to prevent fraud/harm
- **Business transfers** (merger/acquisition) with notice where required

## 5. Where data is stored

Account and habit data are stored on our cloud backend so you can access Patterns across sessions and devices. Your browser/device may also keep a temporary offline cache for sync. This is **not** a device-only / local-only app.

## 6. Security & breach notice

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data (encryption in transit, access controls, account isolation). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

If a breach of personal information affecting Missouri residents occurs as defined by Mo. Rev. Stat. § 407.1500, we will provide notice without unreasonable delay consistent with that statute and any law-enforcement delay exceptions.

## 7. Retention & deletion

We keep account and habit data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, disputes, and legal compliance. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify personal data we control, except where retention is required by law or needed for security/investigations.

## 8. Your choices & rights

- Access and update profile data in Account / Settings
- Disable notifications, push, or calendar sync at any time
- Delete your account in Settings / Account (Danger Zone)
- Request privacy help by email (below)

If you are a resident of a state with additional statutory privacy rights (for example California), we will honor applicable verified requests to the extent required by that law.

## 9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Health & sensitive data note

Habit tracking can include wellness-related routines you choose. Patterns is a consumer wellness tool, not a covered healthcare provider under HIPAA in ordinary use. Do not use the Service to store highly sensitive medical records you would not want associated with a consumer app.

## 11. International users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access it from another country, you understand your information may be processed in the U.S.

## 12. Changes

We may update this Policy and will post the new effective date here. Material changes may also be highlighted in-app or by email when appropriate.
