# Cookie & Local Storage Policy

**Effective:** July 26, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 26, 2026  

This policy explains how Patterns uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies in the web app. It complements our [Privacy Policy](./privacy.md).

## 1. What we use

- **Essential / auth** — session tokens and security settings required to keep you signed in and protect your account
- **Preferences** — theme, collapsed sections, onboarding dismissals, and similar UI state stored locally on your device
- **Offline / sync** — cached habit data and offline queues so check-ins can work without a network, scoped to your account
- **Analytics** — product analytics (for example PostHog) may set cookies or local identifiers to understand feature usage and reliability. We use this to improve the Service, not to sell personal information.

## 2. Your choices

- You can clear site data in your browser settings at any time
- Signing out clears Patterns local account caches we control
- Blocking all cookies may prevent login or break core features — essential storage is required for the app to function
